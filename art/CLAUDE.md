# art/

## Architecture
- `duelyst/units/*.png + *.tres`: unit sprite sheets and their SpriteFrames (animations: idle, attack, hit, death, run, breathing).
- `duelyst/icons/*.png + *.tres`: artifact and spell icons used by items and relics.
- Source: https://github.com/Jordyfel/duelyst-animated-sprites-godot (CC0, see `duelyst/LICENSE`).
  Copied as plain files, not as an addon. The `.tres` paths were rewritten to `res://art/duelyst/...` and uids stripped.

## Etiquette
- Copy in only what a catalog uses. Keep the original file names.
